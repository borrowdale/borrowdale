## Incorporated code

The drawing engine includes work derived from rough.js, hachure-fill, points-on-curve, points-on-path, path-data-parser and fdlibm. The chart tick logic incorporates work from d3-array, and the hierarchy layouts are ported from d3-hierarchy.

## Example datasets

The three dashboards use saved snapshots retrieved on 29 October 2026. They retain source URLs and provide data downloads.

- **World Bank:** World Development Indicators, CC BY 4.0. The example uses selected countries and indicators, not a complete global dataset. [Dataset source](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/infrastructure-data/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators).
- **NASA GISS:** GISTEMP v4 global land–ocean temperature anomalies. The source states no data licence; it asks for credit to NASA's Goddard Institute for Space Studies (or NASA GISS/GISTEMP) and for citations of the GISTEMP page, with its access date, and of [Lenssen et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1029/2023JD040179). [Dataset source](https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/).
- **USGS:** M4.5+ 30-day earthquake feed, USGS-produced public-domain data. [Feed documentation](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/geojson.php).
