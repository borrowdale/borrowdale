Colour cells by value through a chart's new `colorAxis`, with sequential, diverging, quantize, quantile and threshold scales and a ramp or class legend. Draw heatmaps on band or continuous axes, punch cards, hexagon heatmaps and correlation matrices, polar and radar heatmaps, calendar heatmaps over any date range, and horizon charts.

A count cell of a `histogram2d` or `hexbin` series now keeps its hachure density when the chart's or the series' `style` sets `fillStyle` or `hachureGap`.
