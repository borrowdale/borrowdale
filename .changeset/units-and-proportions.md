"@borrowdale/charts": minor

Add charts of parts and units. A `units` series draws each category as a count of equal units: a waffle, the seats of a parliament, rows of pictograms, or a segmented doughnut. A `funnel` series draws stages in turn: a funnel, back to back with another, a pyramid, a funnel area, or the bands of a silhouette given as SVG path data. A `liquid` series shows a level as liquid in a circle or a rectangle. A pie's `breakout` draws one slice's parts as a second pie, a band axis' `weights: "stacks"` sizes a Marimekko chart's columns by their stacks, and a bar's `shape: "pictorial"` repeats a symbol along it.
