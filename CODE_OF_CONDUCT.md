# Code of conduct

BorrowdaleJS wants taking part to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their background,
identity or level of experience. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
space where people take part in the project, and to anyone representing it elsewhere.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and patient, and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, specific and actionable.
- Accept that people disagree, and that a maintainer's decision may differ from yours.
- Say so when you are wrong, and help others when they are.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or comments that demean people for who they are.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Threats, or encouraging others to harm someone.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting conversations, or continuing after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to conduct@borrowdalejs.com. Reports are kept confidential, and
the person reporting is not identified to others without their agreement. If a report concerns a
maintainer, say so: that maintainer takes no part in handling it wherever another maintainer can.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban a participant, depending on how serious
and how repeated the behaviour is. The aim is to stop the harm and keep the project welcoming, not to
punish.

This code draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
